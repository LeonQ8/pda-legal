# Support — Pirate Duel Arena

**Contact:** PirateDuelArena.help@outlook.com. Please include your **captain ID** (Profile screen) and your device model.

## Common questions
- **I bought gems but didn't get them.** Open the Store → Gems tab → **Restore Purchases** (or Settings → Account →
  Restore Purchases). Purchases are never lost: the game delivers them as soon as it reaches the server.
- **"Waiting for approval".** The purchase needs approval (Ask to Buy, or your bank). The gems arrive by themselves
  once it's approved.
- **New phone / reinstalled the game.** If you used **Sign in with Apple** or **Game Center**, open Settings → Account
  and sign in again (Game Center does it automatically). The game offers "Continue as" with your captain's name.
- **Refunds.** Refunds are handled by Apple: https://reportaproblem.apple.com.
- **Cancel the Admiral's Pass.** Settings on your device → your name → Subscriptions → Admiral's Pass.
- **Delete my account.** Settings → Account → Delete account (type DELETE). It's immediate and final.
- **Report a player.** Tap their name → player card → Report.
