# Privacy Policy — Pirate Duel Arena

_Last updated: 25 October 2026_

Pirate Duel Arena ("the game") is made by the developer of Pirate Duel Arena ("we"). This policy explains what the
game stores about you, why, and what you can do about it. Short version: **no ads, no tracking, no selling of data.**
We keep what the game needs to work, and nothing else.

## What we store
| What | Why | Where it comes from |
|---|---|---|
| A game account id and a random id for your device | to keep your captain between sessions | created by the game |
| If you sign in: an Apple ID user number (never your name or e-mail) and/or your Game Center player id | to bring your captain back on a new phone or after a reinstall | Sign in with Apple / Game Center, only when you use them |
| A Sign in with Apple token | so we can tell Apple to remove the connection when you delete your account | Apple, when you sign in |
| Your captain: name, look, level, Fame, gold, gems, items, quests, match results and statistics | the game itself | your play |
| Social data: rivals, blocked captains, preset messages you send and receive, reports you make | the social features and player safety | your play |
| Purchases: App Store transaction ids, what was bought, when, the App Store country, refunds | to deliver what you bought exactly once, and to handle refunds | Apple's signed purchase records |
| Your App Store country (for example "Kuwait") | the Country leaderboard | the App Store, not your GPS |
| Technical logs on our server (for example the time of a request and errors) | to keep the game running and fix bugs | our server, kept for 30 days |

We **don't** collect your e-mail, phone number, contacts, precise location, photos, advertising id or browsing history.
The game has no ads and no analytics or tracking tools.

## Who else sees it
- **Other players** see what a game shows: your captain's name, look, level, Fame, country, captain ID and online
  status. They never see your Apple or Game Center ids.
- **Apple** handles payments, Sign in with Apple and Game Center under Apple's own privacy policy. If you ask Apple
  for a refund **and you said yes in the game** (Settings → Account → "Refund info for Apple"), we tell Apple whether
  the bought gems were already used, so Apple can decide fairly. If you said no, we tell Apple nothing.
- **Our hosting provider** stores the server and its backups for us (named here before the game's public launch).
- We don't sell or share your data with anyone else, except when the law requires it.

## How long we keep it
As long as your account exists. When you delete your account, your captain, wallet, items, social data and
leaderboard entries are deleted at once, and your Apple sign-in is revoked. Purchase records stay **without anything
that identifies you**, because Apple may still send refunds for them and accounting rules ask us to keep sales records.
Backups are overwritten after 30 days.

## Your choices and rights
- **Delete your account:** in the game, Settings → Account → Delete account. It's immediate and can't be undone.
- **Refund info for Apple:** on or off in Settings → Account, any time.
- **Ask us** for a copy of your data or a correction: PirateDuelArena.help@outlook.com, with your captain ID (Profile screen).
- Depending on where you live (for example the EU/UK GDPR or California), you may have more rights. Write to us and we
  will help.

## Children
The game is rated 9+. It has no free-text chat (only preset messages), and it is not made for children under 13. We
don't knowingly collect more than the table above from anyone.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the new version is posted here with a new date. Important changes are also shown in the game.
